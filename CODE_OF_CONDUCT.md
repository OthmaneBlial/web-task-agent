# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private data, credentials, and security reports.
- Welcome questions from newcomers and explain decisions with evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Encouraging unsafe, illegal, or access-control-bypassing automation.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring maintainer boundaries.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as fairly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
